THE PHILOSOPHY

Why Phone Apps Fail for Motorcycles — and Why AMTIS Exists

Every rider wants a log of their best rides, canyon trips, and track days. But current tracking solutions are flawed, frustrating, or downright dangerous for motorbikes.

The Problem with Phone Apps

  • Camera sensor damage: Handlebar vibration destroys optical image stabilization (OIS) on expensive phones within months.
  • Battery drain & overheating: Running 1Hz GPS and screen in direct sunlight overheats phones and drains the battery fast.
  • Glove friction & forgetting: You forget to press "Start Ride", or have to remove thick leather gloves just to tap a screen at a gas station.
  • Sluggish 1Hz GPS: Standard phones sample GPS once every second. At 100 km/h, you travel 28 meters between points, cutting corners and losing precision.

The AMTIS Zero-Touch Solution

  • Tied to ignition power: Turns ON when you start the bike, turns OFF when you park. Never touch a button or charge a separate gadget.
  • True 10Hz high-frequency GPS: Samples position and speed 10 times per second (every 2.8 meters at 100 km/h) for razor-sharp telemetry lines.
  • Automatic home Wi-Fi sync: When you pull into your driveway or garage, your rides automatically upload to your private cloud before you unbuckle your helmet.
  • Zero parasitic battery drain: Uses no power whatsoever when the bike is parked. Your motorcycle battery remains 100% fresh all winter.
SEAMLESS WORKFLOW

How AMTIS Works in Daily Riding

From the moment you start your engine to the moment you review your route on your couch.

1

Turn Ignition ON

The USB-C power rail on your motorcycle powers the tracker. The u-blox M10 GPS module locks onto satellites in under 2 seconds. No apps to open, no Bluetooth pairing.

2

Ride Anywhere

Every twist, speed change, and straightaway is captured at 10Hz and securely saved to solid-state flash memory. Even if you ride through areas with no cellular signal, nothing is lost.

3

Park & View

When you park near your home, the tracker connects to your Wi-Fi, uploads the ride files to your cloud server, and powers off safely with the ignition. Open your laptop and your ride is already waiting!
