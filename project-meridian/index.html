The Core Problem

Conventional productivity tools make you busier, not clearer.

Most apps assume the problem is forgetting tasks. The deeper problem is disorientation. When a task is not connected to a chosen altitude, completion feels empty and non-completion feels like personal failure.

Guilt spirals

A missed task becomes evidence that you are behind. Shame triggers avoidance, and avoidance grows the backlog.

Overwhelming backlogs

Infinite capture rewards accumulation. The list becomes a museum of obligations instead of a compass for meaningful action.

Avoidance framing

“Don’t eat junk,” “stop procrastinating,” and “quit scrolling” keep attention fixed on the unwanted behavior instead of a chosen destination.

Missing altitude

Daily tasks float free of one-year objectives and ten-year meaning, so urgency masquerades as importance.

Infinite backlog → Missed task → Shame → Avoidance → Larger backlog ↺

Approach-Goal Guard

Turn “stop” into “toward.”

Shiny Object Incubator

Every idea is welcome. Not every idea is ready.

New inspiration can destroy an in-flight objective. The Incubator quarantines shiny objects for seven days, then asks four synthesis questions and issues a clear verdict.

Day 0 — Capture without commitment

The idea is safely stored, so your mind can release it. Nothing is lost, but nothing is allowed to hijack the current quarter either.

Day 1 — Let excitement cool

Novelty is chemically compelling, but it is not evidence of importance. The first day separates the rush of discovery from durable value.

Day 3 — Test altitude alignment

Meridian asks whether the idea serves the current one-year objective, quarterly season, and weekly rhythm—or merely offers escape from them.

Day 5 — Four synthesis questions

  1. 1Does this serve my current altitude, or only my current mood?
  2. 2What would it replace if I integrated it now?
  3. 3What is the smallest useful version?
  4. 4Is it still alive after the excitement has cooled?

Day 7 — The verdict

The idea receives a clean decision. No backlog guilt. No silent accumulation. Only intentional integration, future extension, or respectful retirement.

Integrate

The idea aligns with the current altitude. Convert it into a bounded project, habit, or next action.

Extend

The idea has life, but this is the wrong season. Schedule it for a future quarterly review.

Retire

The idea no longer holds energy or meaning. Release it cleanly, without guilt or open loops.

Questions

What makes Meridian different?

Is Project Meridian just another to-do app? +
No. Tasks may exist, but they are not the center. Meridian begins with meaning, then descends through nine altitudes until one golden daily action becomes obvious.
Do I have to complete all nine discovery sessions at once? +
No. The cascade can be completed gradually. Even a rough ten-year horizon and one-year objective can give today’s golden thread meaningful context.
What happens when I miss a day? +
Meridian does not punish you with a broken streak. The system treats the missed day as feedback, then helps you choose the next aligned action.
Will the Shiny Object Incubator make me lose good ideas? +
No. Ideas are captured safely and revisited after seven days. The incubator prevents impulsive context switching while still giving promising ideas a structured review.
How private is my data? +
Meridian uses WebAuthn passkeys, Google Authenticator TOTP backup, encrypted local-first storage, and automated offsite R2 snapshots. Your reflections are not treated as advertising material.
Does it work on mobile? +
Yes. Pocket Mode is designed specifically as a calm mobile morning ritual with touch-friendly controls and a single-screen anchor.
